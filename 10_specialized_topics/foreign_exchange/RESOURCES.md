## 📖 Textbooks

### *The Microstructure Approach to Exchange Rates* — Richard Lyons
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Richard Lyons (Berkeley Haas)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (MIT Press)
- **Covers:** FX market structure, dealer behavior, order flow, price discovery, FX volatility, carry trade microstructure
- **Notes:** The definitive academic text on FX market microstructure. Essential for understanding how exchange rates are determined by order flow rather than macroeconomic fundamentals alone.

### *International Finance: Theory and Policy* — Paul Krugman, Maurice Obstfeld & Marc Melitz
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Krugman, Obstfeld & Melitz (Pearson)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Exchange rate determination, PPP, interest rate parity, balance of payments, currency crises, international monetary policy
- **Notes:** The standard international finance and macroeconomics textbook. Provides the macro framework underlying FX valuation models used in quant finance.
