##  Textbooks

### [Asset Pricing — John Cochrane (with lecture notes)](https://www.johnhcochrane.com/asset-pricing)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John Cochrane (Hoover Institution / University of Chicago)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** Partial (lecture slides freely available on author's website; book via Princeton UP)
- **Covers:** Consumption-based models, Lucas tree model, habit formation, long-run risk, rare disasters, term premium, equity premium puzzle
- **Notes:** Chapters on macroeconomic asset pricing are essential for this module. Cochrane's website provides free lecture notes and course materials.

### *The Theory of Corporate Finance* — Jean Tirole
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Jean Tirole (Toulouse School of Economics, Nobel Laureate)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** Capital structure, liquidity, corporate governance, financial intermediation, systemic risk, macroprudential regulation
- **Notes:** The graduate-level reference for macro-finance and financial intermediation theory. Essential for understanding how financial frictions affect the macroeconomy.

### *Monetary Policy, Inflation, and the Business Cycle* — Jordi Galí
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Jordi Galí (CREI / Universitat Pompeu Fabra)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** New Keynesian framework, DSGE models, monetary policy rules, liquidity traps, financial frictions in macro
- **Notes:** The standard graduate macroeconomics reference for understanding monetary policy in macro-finance models. Essential for FINM 35000/35900 level macro-finance.
