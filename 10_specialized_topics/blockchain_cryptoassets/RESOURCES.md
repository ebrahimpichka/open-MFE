##  Textbooks

### [Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies — Narayanan, Bonneau, Felten, Miller & Goldfeder (Princeton)](https://bitcoinbook.cs.princeton.edu/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Princeton University
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Cryptographic primitives, Bitcoin protocol, consensus mechanisms, smart contracts, anonymity, blockchain applications
- **Notes:** The Princeton textbook on cryptocurrencies, freely available as a PDF. Rigorous, academic treatment. The best open-access starting point for understanding the technology underlying cryptoassets.

### *Mastering Bitcoin* — Andreas M. Antonopoulos
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Andreas M. Antonopoulos (O'Reilly)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Yes (2nd edition freely available on [GitHub](https://github.com/bitcoinbook/bitcoinbook))
- **Covers:** Bitcoin protocol, transactions, scripting, mining, wallets, network security
- **Notes:** The definitive technical guide to Bitcoin. Freely available on GitHub under open license. Essential for understanding the protocol layer of crypto finance.

### *Mastering Ethereum* — Andreas M. Antonopoulos & Gavin Wood
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Antonopoulos & Wood (O'Reilly)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Yes (freely available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ethereumbook/ethereumbook))
- **Covers:** Ethereum protocol, smart contracts, Solidity, decentralized applications (DApps), DeFi foundations
- **Notes:** The definitive Ethereum guide, freely available on GitHub. Essential for understanding smart contract infrastructure underlying DeFi applications.
