##  Textbooks

### [Speech and Language Processing (3rd ed. draft) — Jurafsky & Martin (LLM chapters)](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Daniel Jurafsky & James H. Martin (Stanford)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Transformer architecture, BERT, GPT-style models, instruction tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, LLM agents
- **Notes:** Freely available at web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3. Latest chapters cover LLMs and agents directly applicable to finance use cases (document extraction, sentiment, report generation).

### *Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)* — Sebastian Raschka
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Sebastian Raschka (Manning)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Partial (code freely available on [GitHub](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch); book for purchase)
- **Covers:** Transformer architecture from scratch, attention mechanisms, tokenization, pretraining, fine-tuning, instruction tuning
- **Notes:** Hands-on guide to building GPT-style LLMs from scratch in PyTorch. Essential for understanding how to adapt LLMs for financial document processing and analysis.
