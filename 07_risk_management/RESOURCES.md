## 📖 Textbooks

### *Quantitative Risk Management: Concepts, Techniques, and Tools* — Alexander McNeil, Rüdiger Frey & Paul Embrechts
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** McNeil, Frey & Embrechts (Princeton University Press)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** Risk measures (VaR, Expected Shortfall), extreme value theory, copulas, dependence modeling, market risk, credit risk, operational risk, Basel regulations
- **Notes:** The gold standard quantitative risk management reference. Essential for MFE programs. QRM Tutorial website (qrmtutorial.org) provides free R code and exercise solutions as a companion resource.

### *Risk Management and Financial Institutions* — John C. Hull
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John C. Hull (Rotman School of Management)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** VaR, expected shortfall, Basel accords, market risk, credit risk, operational risk, liquidity risk, stress testing, counterparty risk
- **Notes:** Accessible introduction to the full risk management landscape. Less mathematical than McNeil-Frey-Embrechts but broader in regulatory and institutional coverage. Excellent complement.

### *Value at Risk: The New Benchmark for Managing Financial Risk* — Philippe Jorion
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Philippe Jorion (UC Irvine)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (McGraw-Hill)
- **Covers:** VaR computation methods, backtesting, stress testing, market risk, credit VaR, regulatory capital
- **Notes:** The foundational text on Value at Risk. Remains the standard practitioner reference for market risk despite the industry's shift towards Expected Shortfall.
