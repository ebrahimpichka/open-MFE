##  Textbooks

### *Active Portfolio Management* — Richard Grinold & Ronald Kahn
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Richard Grinold & Ronald Kahn (McGraw-Hill)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Fundamental Law of Active Management, alpha generation, risk modeling, portfolio construction, performance attribution, transaction costs
- **Notes:** The definitive quantitative active management reference. The Fundamental Law of Active Management (IR = IC × √BR) is a cornerstone concept for all systematic investment managers.

### *Quantitative Equity Portfolio Management* — Ludwig Chincarini & Daehwan Kim
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Chincarini & Kim (McGraw-Hill)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Factor models, alpha factor research, portfolio optimization, transaction cost analysis, backtesting, performance evaluation
- **Notes:** Comprehensive practitioner-level text on quantitative equity investing. Strong on implementation details from factor research to live trading.
