# 8.1 — Portfolio Theory & Asset Pricing

> **Reference Programs:** Berkeley MFE 230A, CMU 46972, UChicago FINM 36700

## Topics Covered
- Markowitz mean-variance optimization
- CAPM, multi-factor models (Fama-French)
- Performance attribution

---

##  Textbooks

### [Asset Pricing — John Cochrane](https://www.johnhcochrane.com/asset-pricing)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John Cochrane (Hoover Institution / University of Chicago)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** Partial (lecture notes and course materials freely available on Cochrane's website)
- **Covers:** Consumption-based models, CAPM, factor pricing, GMM estimation, option pricing, bond pricing, stochastic discount factors
- **Notes:** The canonical graduate asset pricing textbook. Cochranes website provides free lecture slides and course notes. The stochastic discount factor framework unifies all asset pricing models.

### *The Econometrics of Financial Markets* — John Y. Campbell, Andrew W. Lo & A. Craig MacKinlay
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Campbell, Lo & MacKinlay (Princeton University Press)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** CAPM tests, factor models, event studies, market microstructure, volatility models, term structure, derivative pricing
- **Notes:** The empirical finance bible. Essential for understanding how asset pricing theories are tested. Strong coverage of statistical methodology.

### *Active Portfolio Management* — Richard Grinold & Ronald Kahn
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Richard Grinold & Ronald Kahn (McGraw-Hill)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Fundamental Law of Active Management, information ratio, alpha signals, portfolio construction, risk models, performance attribution
- **Notes:** The "bible" of quantitative active management. Introduces the framework used by most systematic equity managers.
