# 8.3 — Financial Optimization

`CMU: 46976 | UChicago: FINM 34800`

##  Courses & Lectures

- **[Stanford EE364A — Convex Optimization (Stephen Boyd)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMJqxxviPa4AmDClvcbHi6h)**
  The foundational convex optimization course. LP, QP, SOCP, SDP, duality, KKT conditions. Essential for portfolio optimization.

- **[DTU 42123 — Optimization in Finance](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMn2aW3wpAtOK-V5tt2FzNH1swJVLr9PU)**
  Finance-specific optimization: mean-variance, robust optimization, stochastic programming, scenario-based methods.

##  Textbooks

- **[Optimization Methods in Finance — Cornuejols & Tutuncu](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/gc0v/webpub/OptFinFirstEdition2006.pdf)**
  First edition freely available. LP, QP, integer programming, stochastic programming applied to finance.
  [Second edition](https://www.cambridge.org/ca/universitypress/subjects/mathematics/mathematical-finance/optimization-methods-finance-2nd-edition) (Cambridge University Press)
