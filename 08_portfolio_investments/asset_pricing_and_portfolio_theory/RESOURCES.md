##  Textbooks

### [Asset Pricing — John Cochrane](https://www.johnhcochrane.com/asset-pricing)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John Cochrane (Hoover Institution / University of Chicago)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** Partial (lecture slides and notes freely available on author's website)
- **Covers:** CAPM, APT, factor pricing, stochastic discount factors, option pricing, bond pricing, GMM
- **Notes:** The graduate asset pricing standard. Used at UChicago's FINM 36700. The SDF framework is essential for understanding modern asset pricing theory.

### *Investments* — Zvi Bodie, Alex Kane & Alan Marcus
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Bodie, Kane & Marcus (McGraw-Hill)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Portfolio theory, CAPM, APT, efficient markets, equity and bond valuation, options, futures, performance evaluation
- **Notes:** The comprehensive standard investments textbook. Used in CFA preparation and MFE programs as the accessible starting reference for asset pricing and portfolio theory.

### *Expected Returns: An Investor's Guide to Harvesting Market Rewards* — Antti Ilmanen
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Antti Ilmanen (AQR)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Equity risk premium, bond risk premium, carry trades, value and momentum, volatility premium, alternative risk premia
- **Notes:** Comprehensive empirical review of return predictability and risk premia across asset classes. Bridges academic factor research and institutional investment practice.
