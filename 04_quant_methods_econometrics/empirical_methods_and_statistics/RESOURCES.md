## 📖 Textbooks

### [Econometrics — Bruce E. Hansen (UWisconsin)](https://www.ssc.wisc.edu/~bhansen/econometrics/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Bruce E. Hansen (University of Wisconsin-Madison)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** OLS, GLS, IV, GMM, panel data, time series, bootstrap, asymptotic theory
- **Notes:** Freely available graduate econometrics textbook from a leading econometrician. Rigorous and comprehensive — widely used in doctoral programs and increasingly in MFE contexts.

### *Econometric Analysis* — William H. Greene
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** William H. Greene (NYU Stern)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Pearson)
- **Covers:** Linear regression, MLE, GMM, panel data, discrete choice models, simulation
- **Notes:** The standard graduate econometrics reference. Dense and comprehensive. Covers the full toolkit used in empirical finance research.

### *Introductory Econometrics for Finance* — Chris Brooks
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Chris Brooks (ICMA Centre)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Cambridge University Press)
- **Covers:** OLS, diagnostic tests, time series (ARMA, GARCH), panel data, event studies, cointegration
- **Notes:** Designed specifically for finance students. The most accessible graduate econometrics text for MFE programs. Uses EViews and R throughout.
