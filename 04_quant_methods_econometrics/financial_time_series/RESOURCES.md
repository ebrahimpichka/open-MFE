##  Textbooks

### [Forecasting: Principles and Practice (3rd ed.) — Rob Hyndman & George Athanasopoulos](https://otexts.com/fpp3/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Rob Hyndman & George Athanasopoulos (Monash University)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** ARIMA, exponential smoothing, regression, time series decomposition, state-space models, forecasting accuracy
- **Notes:** Freely available online at otexts.com/fpp3. Modern, practical treatment with R code. Excellent for mastering time series fundamentals before moving to financial applications.

### *Time Series Analysis* — James D. Hamilton
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** James D. Hamilton (UC San Diego)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** ARMA, VAR, Kalman filter, ARCH/GARCH, cointegration, spectral analysis, state-space models
- **Notes:** The "bible" of time series analysis in economics and finance. Exhaustive reference covering all major models. Hamilton provides supplementary data and code on his UCSD website.

### *Analysis of Financial Time Series* — Ruey S. Tsay
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Ruey S. Tsay (University of Chicago Booth)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** ARIMA, GARCH, multivariate time series, high-frequency data, value at risk, copulas, neural networks for time series
- **Notes:** Finance-specific time series text with strong R coverage. Aligns directly with Baruch MTH 9875 and CMU 46929 syllabi.
