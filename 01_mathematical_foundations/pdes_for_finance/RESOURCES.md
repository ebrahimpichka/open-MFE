# 1.2 — PDEs for Finance

`Berkeley: MFE 230D | CMU: 46932 | Baruch: MTH 9833`

## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems — Prof. Serge Richard, Nagoya University](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZ2qogva1kkYC-EcPfme0GeK2fKl27kie)**
  Rigorous treatment of ODEs and PDEs. Covers parabolic equations and their connection to stochastic processes via Feynman-Kac.

- **[MIT Learn Differential Equations — Gilbert Strang & Cleve Moler](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP63oTpyxCMLKt_JmB0WtSZfG)**
  Accessible, visual approach to ODEs and PDEs. Good foundation before tackling the Black-Scholes PDE.
