##  Courses & Lectures

- **[Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems — Prof. Serge Richard, Nagoya University](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZ2qogva1kkYC-EcPfme0GeK2fKl27kie)**
  Rigorous treatment of ODEs and PDEs. Covers parabolic equations and their connection to stochastic processes via Feynman-Kac.

- **[MIT Learn Differential Equations — Gilbert Strang & Cleve Moler](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP63oTpyxCMLKt_JmB0WtSZfG)**
  Accessible, visual approach to ODEs and PDEs. Good foundation before tackling the Black-Scholes PDE.

##  Textbooks

### *The Mathematics of Financial Derivatives: A Student Introduction* — Paul Wilmott, Sam Howison & Jeff Dewynne
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Wilmott, Howison & Dewynne (Cambridge University Press)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Black-Scholes PDE derivation, boundary conditions, American options, barrier options, exotic options, finite difference methods
- **Notes:** The standard introduction to the PDE approach to derivatives pricing. Accessible applied mathematics treatment — bridges calculus to the Black-Scholes PDE without requiring graduate-level analysis.

### *Finite Difference Methods in Financial Engineering* — Daniel J. Duffy
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Daniel J. Duffy (Datasim)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** FDM schemes (explicit, implicit, Crank-Nicolson), stability analysis, multi-factor models, C++ implementation
- **Notes:** The definitive reference for numerical PDE methods in quantitative finance. Essential for anyone implementing finite difference option pricers.

### *Partial Differential Equations* — Lawrence C. Evans
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Lawrence C. Evans (UC Berkeley)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (AMS)
- **Covers:** Sobolev spaces, elliptic equations, parabolic equations, hyperbolic equations, calculus of variations
- **Notes:** The canonical graduate PDE text. Required for rigorous understanding of the mathematical foundations of the Black-Scholes and heat equation. Standard reference in mathematically oriented MFE programs.
