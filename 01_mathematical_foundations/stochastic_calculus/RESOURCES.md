# 1.1 — Stochastic Calculus

`Berkeley: MFE 230Q | CMU: 46944, 46945 | UChicago: FINM 34000, FINM 34500 | Baruch: MTH 9831, MTH 9832`

Covers the full arc: measure-theoretic probability → martingales → Itô calculus → change of measure → risk-neutral pricing.

## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[Introduction to Stochastic Calculus (Fall 2023) — Prof. Serge Richard, Nagoya University](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZ2qogva1kkYDu-qsFzn2htV5TUncOEZu)**
  Rigorous, graduate-level. Covers filtrations, martingales, Brownian motion, Itô integral, Itô's Lemma, SDEs, Girsanov.
  [Course website](https://www.math.nagoya-u.ac.jp/~richard/SMLfall2023.html)

- **[Probability and Stochastics for Finance — Prof. Joydeep Dutta (NPTEL)](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEYrMI37wMbplhGJmqhlYv0VUSwC6zMsU)**
  Accessible entry point. Probability theory through stochastic processes with explicit finance motivation.

- **[Probability and Stochastics for Finance II — Prof. Joydeep Dutta (NPTEL)](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQtOp02o5t8VOjzts8q0rHXLNYHSqdiml)**
  Continuation: Itô calculus, Black-Scholes via SDEs, risk-neutral pricing, change of measure.

## 📖 Textbooks

### *Stochastic Calculus for Finance I: The Binomial Asset Pricing Model* — Steven Shreve
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Steven Shreve (Carnegie Mellon University)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Springer)
- **Covers:** Binomial asset pricing, conditional expectations, martingales, risk-neutral pricing
- **Notes:** Volume I of the MFE gold standard. Builds rigorous probabilistic intuition using the discrete model before moving to continuous time. Prescribed in virtually every top MFE program.

### *Stochastic Calculus for Finance II: Continuous-Time Models* — Steven Shreve
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Steven Shreve (Carnegie Mellon University)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Springer)
- **Covers:** Brownian motion, Itô integral, Itô's Lemma, risk-neutral pricing, change of measure (Girsanov), SDEs, term structure models
- **Notes:** Volume II — the definitive continuous-time finance text. The standard reference at Berkeley MFE, CMU, UChicago, and Baruch programs.

### *Stochastic Differential Equations: An Introduction with Applications* — Bernt Øksendal
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Bernt Øksendal (University of Oslo)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Springer)
- **Covers:** Itô calculus, martingales, SDEs, stochastic control, applications to finance
- **Notes:** Concise, mathematically rigorous treatment of SDE theory. Less finance-centric than Shreve but excellent for understanding the pure mathematics underlying stochastic processes.

### *Arbitrage Theory in Continuous Time* — Tomas Björk
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Tomas Björk (Stockholm School of Economics)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Oxford University Press)
- **Covers:** No-arbitrage theory, risk-neutral pricing, Black-Scholes, term structure models, stochastic control
- **Notes:** Frequently cited by practitioners for its exceptional intuition and clear structure. Strong coverage of interest rate models and HJM framework.

### *Stochastic Calculus and Financial Applications* — J. Michael Steele
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** J. Michael Steele (Wharton, UPenn)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Springer)
- **Covers:** Martingales, Brownian motion, Itô integral, Black-Scholes, optimal stopping
- **Notes:** More accessible and elegant than Shreve Vol. II while remaining rigorous. Excellent for self-study; shows the "beauty" of stochastic calculus.
