## 📖 Textbooks

### *Interest Rate Models — Theory and Practice* — Damiano Brigo & Fabio Mercurio
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Damiano Brigo & Fabio Mercurio
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Springer)
- **Covers:** Caps, floors, swaptions, market models (LMM/BGM), calibration, LIBOR transition, interest rate exotics
- **Notes:** The definitive reference for interest rate derivatives. The LIBOR Market Model chapters are particularly important for swaption pricing and calibration.

### *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* — John C. Hull
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John C. Hull (Rotman School of Management)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** No (Pearson)
- **Covers:** Interest rate futures, swaps, caps, floors, swaptions, bond options, short-rate models
- **Notes:** Accessible first reference for interest rate derivatives before moving to Brigo & Mercurio. Essential foundation for understanding swap/cap/floor mechanics.

### *Interest Rate Modeling* (3 volumes) — Leif Andersen & Vladimir Piterbarg
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Leif Andersen & Vladimir Piterbarg (Atlantic Financial Press)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Term structure models, LIBOR market models, stochastic volatility IR models, smile modeling, multi-curve frameworks
- **Notes:** The most comprehensive modern reference for practitioners. Essential for quantitative interest rate derivatives roles.
