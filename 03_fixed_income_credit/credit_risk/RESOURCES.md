##  Textbooks

### *Credit Risk Modeling: Theory and Applications* — David Lando
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** David Lando (Copenhagen Business School)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** Structural models (Merton, Black-Cox), reduced-form/intensity models, credit derivatives, CDOs, empirical methods
- **Notes:** Rigorous academic treatment bridging theory and practice. The definitive reference for structural and intensity-based credit models in MFE programs.

### *Credit Risk: Pricing, Measurement, and Management* — Darrell Duffie & Kenneth Singleton
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Darrell Duffie & Kenneth Singleton (Stanford GSB)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Princeton University Press)
- **Covers:** Default risk, risk-neutral credit risk measurement, credit derivatives, reduced-form models, empirical credit risk
- **Notes:** Foundational academic text by leading researchers. Strong on the no-arbitrage treatment of credit and its connection to fixed income markets.

### *Credit Derivatives Pricing Models* — Philipp Schönbucher
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Philipp Schönbucher (ETH Zurich)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** CDS pricing, CDO modeling, copula approaches, intensity models, market models for credit derivatives
- **Notes:** Comprehensive technical reference for credit derivatives pricing and implementation. Especially valuable for structured credit and CDO desk work.
