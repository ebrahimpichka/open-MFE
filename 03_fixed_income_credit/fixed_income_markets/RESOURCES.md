## 📖 Textbooks

### *Fixed Income Securities: Tools for Today's Markets* — Bruce Tuckman & Angel Serrat
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Bruce Tuckman & Angel Serrat
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Bond pricing, yield curves, duration/convexity, repo markets, interest rate risk, securitization, structured products
- **Notes:** The preferred practitioner's text. Balances rigour with real-world trading intuition. Widely used on fixed income desks and in MFE programs.

### *Fixed Income Securities: Valuation, Risk, and Risk Management* — Pietro Veronesi
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Pietro Veronesi (University of Chicago Booth)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Bond mathematics, duration, convexity, yield curve construction, term structure models, mortgage-backed securities, immunization
- **Notes:** The quantitative standard for MFE programs. Strong on statistical and mathematical treatment. Chicago Booth-level rigor; aligns directly with UChicago FINM 37400.

### *The Handbook of Fixed Income Securities* — Frank J. Fabozzi (ed.)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Frank J. Fabozzi
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (McGraw-Hill)
- **Covers:** Bond types, yield analysis, duration/convexity, structured products, credit derivatives, portfolio management
- **Notes:** The encyclopedic "bible" of the fixed income industry. Broad coverage across asset classes. Best used as a reference rather than cover-to-cover reading.
