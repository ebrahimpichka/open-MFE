## 📖 Textbooks

### *Interest Rate Models — Theory and Practice* — Damiano Brigo & Fabio Mercurio
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Damiano Brigo & Fabio Mercurio
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Springer)
- **Covers:** Short-rate models (Vasicek, CIR, Hull-White), market models (LIBOR, Swap), HJM framework, calibration
- **Notes:** The most comprehensive reference on interest rate models. Over 900 pages covering theoretical foundations and practical calibration. Essential for fixed income quants.

### *Arbitrage Theory in Continuous Time* — Tomas Björk
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Tomas Björk (Stockholm School of Economics)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Oxford University Press)
- **Covers:** Term structure theory, HJM framework, short-rate models, affine term structure models, forward rate models
- **Notes:** Excellent theoretical treatment of term structure in a no-arbitrage framework. Part III dedicated to interest rates — strong complement to Brigo & Mercurio.

### *Fixed Income Securities: Tools for Today's Markets* — Bruce Tuckman & Angel Serrat
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Bruce Tuckman & Angel Serrat
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Yield curve fitting, factor models, duration, convexity, term structure theories
- **Notes:** Practitioner-oriented coverage of term structure, complementing the more theoretical Brigo & Mercurio text.
