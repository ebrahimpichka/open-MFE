## 📖 Textbooks

### *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* — Marcos López de Prado
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Marcos López de Prado (Cornell University / AQR)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Financial data structures (tick, volume, dollar bars), labeling methods, feature engineering for alternative data, high-frequency data cleaning
- **Notes:** Chapters 1–2 are essential for understanding how to structure financial data from raw tick feeds. The best single reference for working with high-frequency and alternative data.

### *Empirical Market Microstructure* — Joel Hasbrouck
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Joel Hasbrouck (NYU Stern)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Oxford University Press)
- **Covers:** Trade indicators, bid-ask spreads, adverse selection, price impact, intraday volatility, high-frequency econometrics
- **Notes:** The quantitative reference for analyzing high-frequency data. Covers the econometric tools needed to extract signals from market microstructure data.
