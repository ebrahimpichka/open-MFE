# 6.3 — Deep Learning

`CMU: 46937 | UChicago: FINM 33165`

## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[UWaterloo STAT 940 — Deep Learning (Fall 2023) — Ali Ghodsi](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLehuLRPyt1HxuYpdlW4KevYJVOSDG3DEz)**
  Graduate-level. Strong mathematical foundations: backpropagation, CNNs, RNNs, attention, VAEs, GANs.

- **[NYU Deep Learning — Alfredo Canziani & Yann LeCun](https://atcold.github.io/NYU-DLSP21/)**
  [Spring 2021](https://atcold.github.io/NYU-DLSP21/) · [Fall 2022 selection](https://atcold.github.io/NYU-DLFL22/)
  Cutting-edge content from LeCun's lab. Energy-based models, self-supervised learning, Transformers.

- **[CMU 11-785 — Introduction to Deep Learning](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLp-0K3kfddPxUJzAW0KxNNjGiK_hISFas)**
  One of the most comprehensive DL courses available. Covers MLPs, CNNs, RNNs, attention, seq2seq, generative models.

- **[Foundations of Deep Learning — Soheil Feizi (UMD)](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHgjs9ncvHi80UCSlSvQe-TK_uOyDv_Jf)**
  Theory-focused. Covers optimization landscapes, generalization, expressiveness of neural networks.

- **[TTIC 31230 — Fundamentals of Deep Learning — David McAllester](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLVYTJAasVdWwqIOgf-Lz7GPAgoKBADNyi)**
  [Course page](https://mcallester.github.io/ttic-31230/). Information-theoretic and probabilistic view of deep learning.

- **[DeepMind x UCL — Deep Learning Lecture Series 2021](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLqYmG7hTraZDVH599EItlEWsUOsJbAodm)**
  High-quality lectures from DeepMind researchers. Covers generative models, RL, graph networks.
