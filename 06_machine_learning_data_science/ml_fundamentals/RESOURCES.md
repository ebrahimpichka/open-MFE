##  Textbooks

### [An Introduction to Statistical Learning (with Python/R) — James, Witten, Hastie & Tibshirani](https://www.statlearning.com/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** James, Witten, Hastie & Tibshirani (Stanford)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Linear/logistic regression, resampling, regularization, tree methods, SVMs, unsupervised learning, neural networks
- **Notes:** Freely downloadable at statlearning.com. The gold standard for accessible ML. Available in both R and Python editions. Strongly paired with the Stanford Statistical Learning course above.

### [The Elements of Statistical Learning — Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman (Stanford)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Linear methods, kernel methods, boosting, neural networks, SVMs, random forests, graphical models
- **Notes:** Freely downloadable at hastie.su.domains. The definitive graduate-level ML reference. More rigorous and comprehensive than ISL. Essential for serious MFE ML study.

### *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning* — Christopher M. Bishop
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Christopher Bishop (Microsoft Research)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** Yes (freely available at [microsoft.com](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf))
- **Covers:** Bayesian methods, graphical models, mixture models, EM algorithm, kernel methods, neural networks
- **Notes:** The Bayesian perspective on machine learning. Microsoft Research has released the PDF freely. Essential for probabilistic ML approaches used in financial applications.
