# 6.4 — NLP for Finance

## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[Stanford CS224N — NLP with Deep Learning (2023)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMFqRtEuo6SGjY4XbRIVRd4)**
  The standard graduate NLP course. Word vectors, RNNs, attention, Transformers, BERT, question answering, generation.

- **[Stanford XCS224U — Natural Language Understanding (Spring 2023)](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOwvldxftJTmoR3kRcWkJBp)**
  Covers NLU tasks relevant to finance: sentiment, information extraction, grounding, LLM prompting.

- **[CMU CS 11-711 — Advanced NLP (Fall 2024) — Graham Neubig](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8PYTP1V4I8D4BeyjwWczukWq9d8PNyZp)**
  State-of-the-art NLP: LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, instruction tuning, agents.

- **[UMass CS685 — Advanced NLP — Mohit Iyyer (Spring 2024)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLWnsVgP6CzafDszSy-njjdqnliv5qT0EW)**
  Covers structured prediction, generation, and evaluation methods relevant to financial text analysis.

- **[UT Austin NLP — Greg Durrett (2023–2024)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLofp2YXfp7TZZ5c7HEChs0_wfEfewLDs7)**
  Comprehensive NLP course with strong coverage of language models and applications.
