# 9.4 — High-Frequency Trading

> **Reference Programs:** UChicago FINM 34600, CMU 46982

## Topics Covered
- Market-making models (Avellaneda-Stoikov)
- Latency arbitrage, co-location
- HFT regulation and microstructure noise

---

##  Textbooks

### *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading* — Álvaro Cartea, Sebastián Jaimungal & José Peñalva
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Cartea, Jaimungal & Peñalva (Cambridge University Press)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Market-making models (Avellaneda-Stoikov), latency arbitrage, co-location, HFT regulation, LOB dynamics, stochastic optimal control
- **Notes:** The mathematical standard for HFT. Covers Avellaneda-Stoikov market-making model in depth. Authors have freely available mini-course notes online.

### *Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners* — Larry Harris
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Larry Harris (USC Marshall)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Oxford University Press)
- **Covers:** HFT strategies, liquidity provision, adverse selection, market quality, exchange design
- **Notes:** Foundational text for understanding the institutional structure within which HFT operates. Essential companion to the more mathematical Cartea-Jaimungal text.
