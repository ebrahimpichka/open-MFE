##  Textbooks

### *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* — Marcos López de Prado
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Marcos López de Prado (Cornell University / AQR)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Alpha research process, backtesting, combinatorial purged cross-validation, bet sizing, strategy evaluation
- **Notes:** Essential reading for quantitative strategy development. Fundamentally rethinks backtesting methodology to avoid overfitting and false discoveries.

### *Quantitative Trading* — Ernest Chan
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Ernest Chan (Wiley)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Strategy development, backtesting, mean reversion, momentum, statistical arbitrage, execution, risk management
- **Notes:** Practical guide to building systematic trading strategies. Accessible and implementation-focused. Ideal starting point for MFE students entering quantitative trading.

### *Statistical Arbitrage* — Andrew Pole
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Andrew Pole (Wiley)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Pairs trading, cointegration, mean reversion strategies, risk management, regime changes
- **Notes:** The standard reference for statistical arbitrage strategies. Covers both theoretical foundations and practical implementation.
