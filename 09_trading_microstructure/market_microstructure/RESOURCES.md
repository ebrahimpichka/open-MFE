## 📖 Textbooks

### *Market Microstructure Theory* — Maureen O'Hara
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Maureen O'Hara (Cornell University)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Blackwell)
- **Covers:** Inventory models, asymmetric information models (Kyle, Glosten-Milgrom), bid-ask spread theory, price discovery, market design
- **Notes:** The standard theoretical reference for market microstructure. Essential for understanding the economic models underpinning trading costs and price formation.

### *Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners* — Larry Harris
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Larry Harris (USC Marshall)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (Oxford University Press)
- **Covers:** Order types, market participants, liquidity, spreads, market mechanisms, exchange design, trading costs, HFT, regulation
- **Notes:** The most accessible and comprehensive guide to how markets work in practice. Essential for understanding why market participants trade and how exchanges function.

### *Empirical Market Microstructure* — Joel Hasbrouck
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Joel Hasbrouck (NYU Stern)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Oxford University Press)
- **Covers:** Trade indicators, bid-ask spreads, price impact, intraday patterns, market quality, high-frequency econometrics
- **Notes:** The quantitative/econometric reference for market microstructure. Essential for researchers and practitioners working with high-frequency data.
