## 📖 Textbooks

### *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading* — Álvaro Cartea, Sebastián Jaimungal & José Peñalva
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Cartea, Jaimungal & Peñalva (Cambridge University Press)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Optimal execution (Almgren-Chriss), market making, stochastic optimal control, limit order book dynamics, market impact models
- **Notes:** The mathematical standard for optimal execution and market making. Essential for quantitative trading desks. Authors maintain freely accessible mini-course notes online.

### *Optimal Trading Strategies* — Robert Kissell
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Robert Kissell (AMACOM)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** VWAP, TWAP, implementation shortfall, transaction cost analysis, market impact estimation, algorithmic strategy selection
- **Notes:** Practitioner-oriented text on optimal order execution. Focuses on real-world trading strategy selection and transaction cost measurement.
