## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[Caltech BEM1105x — Pricing Options with Mathematical Models (Prof. Jakša Cvitanić)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8_xPU5epJdfCxbRzxuchTfgOH1I2Ibht)**

- **[Introduction to Derivative Pricing — IIT Bombay, Mathematics Dept. (2020)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLjAj1Z92T7LTcyjV-Di2_a4mij9_5fKkM)**
  Covers the full pricing framework. Especially useful from Lecture 12 onward for risk-neutral pricing, PDE methods, and exotic structures.

## 📖 Textbooks

### *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* — John C. Hull
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John C. Hull (Rotman School of Management)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** No (Pearson)
- **Covers:** Futures, forwards, swaps, options, binomial trees, Black-Scholes model, Greeks, volatility smiles, exotic options, interest rate derivatives
- **Notes:** The "bible" of the derivatives industry. Comprehensive coverage balancing theory and practice. Universally assigned across MFE programs as the first derivatives text.

### *Option Volatility and Pricing* — Sheldon Natenberg
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Sheldon Natenberg
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No (McGraw-Hill)
- **Covers:** Options mechanics, pricing models, volatility concepts, delta hedging, trading strategies, risk management
- **Notes:** Practitioner classic. Highly regarded for its intuitive, trading-desk-oriented approach to options pricing and risk. Essential companion to Hull for those entering trading roles.

### *Paul Wilmott on Quantitative Finance* (3-volume set) — Paul Wilmott
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Paul Wilmott
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Stochastic calculus, Black-Scholes, volatility models, exotic derivatives, numerical methods, fixed income, risk management
- **Notes:** Encyclopedic treatment of quantitative finance from a PDE perspective. Vol. 1–3 cover the full MFE curriculum. Wilmott's own CERTIFICATE program is based on this material.
