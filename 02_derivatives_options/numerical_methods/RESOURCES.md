# 2.2 — Numerical Methods

`Berkeley: MFE 230D | CMU: 46932 | UChicago: FINM 32000 | Baruch: MTH 9821`

## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[Computational Finance Course — Lech A. Grzelak](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6zzGYGhbWrPaI-op1UfNl0uDglxdkaOB)**
  Directly targets quantitative finance: Monte Carlo, finite differences, Fourier methods, calibration. Highly recommended.

- **[Numerical Methods in Computational Finance](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLit15QLE4RaaHhMDtyi-FqZhtyZaHmmo1)**
  Focused playlist covering FDM schemes, Monte Carlo variance reduction, and tree methods applied to option pricing.

- **[Lectures on Computational Finance / Numerical Methods](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJ9XZsVSloaQeqJwoUPQbqa1EjxRfgRUO)**
  Supplementary lecture series on computational methods for derivatives pricing.

- **[MIT 6.7350 — Numerical Algorithms for Computing and Machine Learning (Fall 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQ3UicqQtfNsivZX5TmUAoUkkBqFT8aOL)**
  Instructor: Justin Solomon. Broader numerical methods course (interpolation, integration, linear solvers, optimization). Strong theoretical grounding.

- **[Numerical Methods for Engineers](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLkZjai-2Jcxn35XnijUtqqEg0Wi5Sn8ab)**
  Applied engineering-focused treatment of root finding, interpolation, ODE/PDE solvers, and numerical integration.
