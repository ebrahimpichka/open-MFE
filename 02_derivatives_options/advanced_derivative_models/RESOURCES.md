# 2.2 — Advanced Derivative Models

> **Reference Programs:** CMU 46915, UChicago FINM 34500, Baruch MTH 9853

## Topics Covered
- Local volatility (Dupire), Heston, SABR
- Jump-diffusion (Merton, Kou)
- Variance swaps, model calibration

##  Textbooks

### *The Volatility Surface: A Practitioner's Guide* — Jim Gatheral
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Jim Gatheral (Baruch College)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Local volatility (Dupire), stochastic volatility (Heston, SABR), variance swaps, model calibration, volatility surface fitting
- **Notes:** The definitive practitioner reference on volatility modeling. Essential for anyone working with advanced derivatives models. Gatheral's notes are the basis for Baruch's MTH 9853.

### *Financial Modelling with Jump Processes* — Rama Cont & Peter Tankov
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Rama Cont & Peter Tankov (Chapman & Hall/CRC)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Lévy processes, jump-diffusion models (Merton, Kou), variance gamma, CGMY, model calibration
- **Notes:** The standard reference for jump process models in finance. Rigorous mathematical treatment paired with practical calibration methods.
