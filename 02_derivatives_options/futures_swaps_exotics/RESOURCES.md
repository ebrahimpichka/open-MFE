## 📖 Textbooks

### *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* — John C. Hull
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** John C. Hull (Rotman School of Management)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** No (Pearson)
- **Covers:** Futures mechanics, forward contracts, interest rate swaps, currency swaps, commodity derivatives, exotic options (barriers, Asians, lookbacks)
- **Notes:** The industry-standard reference. Chapters on futures/forwards and swaps are especially strong for practitioners. Universally used in MFE programs.

### *Paul Wilmott on Quantitative Finance* (Volumes 1–3) — Paul Wilmott
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Paul Wilmott
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No (Wiley)
- **Covers:** Exotic option pricing, barrier options, Asian options, lookback options, stochastic volatility, free-boundary problems
- **Notes:** Comprehensive PDE-based treatment of exotic derivatives. Vols. 1–2 cover the majority of exotic structures encountered in practice.
