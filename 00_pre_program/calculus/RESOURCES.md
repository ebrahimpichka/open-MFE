## 🎓 Courses & Lectures

- **[MIT 18.01 — Single Variable Calculus (Fall 2006)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL590CCC2BC5AF3BC1)**
  MIT OpenCourseWare. Covers limits, derivatives, integrals, applications.

- **[Calculus I — Full Course by Dr. Trefor Bazett](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHXZ9OQGMqxfT9RMcReZ4WcoVILP4k6-m)**
  Limits, derivatives, integrals. Excellent pacing and visualizations.

- **[MIT 18.02 — Multivariable Calculus (Fall 2007)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL4C4C8A7D06566F38)**
  MIT OpenCourseWare. Partial derivatives, gradients, multiple integrals, vector calculus.

- **[Oxford Mathematics 1st Year Student Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL4d5ZtfQonW3ZWkdV521ehLkaArdAIiVU)**
  Real analysis focus. Sequences, limits, continuity, differentiation. More rigorous than standard calculus courses.

- **[Multivariable Calculus — The Bright Side of Mathematics](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBh2i93oe2qv4G2AyarkbR3OKBml0hXEg)**
  Concise, proof-oriented. Good supplement for rigorous treatment of multivariable topics.

## 📖 Textbooks

### [CLP Calculus Textbooks — Joel Feldman, Andrew Rechnitzer, Elyse Yeager (UBC)](https://www.math.ubc.ca/~CLP/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** University of British Columbia
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Differential calculus, integral calculus, multivariable calculus, vector calculus
- **Notes:** High-quality open-access calculus sequence used in UBC courses. Clear exposition, well-suited for MFE pre-program preparation.

### [Basic Analysis: Introduction to Real Analysis — Jiří Lebl](https://www.jirka.org/ra/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Jiří Lebl (Oklahoma State University)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Sequences, series, continuity, differentiation, Riemann integration, metric spaces
- **Notes:** Peer-reviewed, widely adopted open-access real analysis text. Balances rigour and readability — ideal bridge before measure-theoretic probability.

### *Calculus* — Michael Spivak
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Michael Spivak
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Rigorous single-variable calculus, limits, derivatives, integration, infinite series
- **Notes:** The gold standard for mathematically rigorous calculus. Develops the intuition for real analysis that underpins stochastic calculus. Highly recommended for MFE students who want deeper foundations.

### *Calculus, Volumes I & II* — Tom M. Apostol
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Tom M. Apostol (Caltech)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Single and multivariable calculus, linear algebra, differential equations
- **Notes:** Classic rigorous treatment, integrating linear algebra with calculus from the start. Excellent preparation for advanced mathematical finance.

### *Principles of Mathematical Analysis* — Walter Rudin
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Walter Rudin
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Real and complex analysis, metric spaces, sequences, continuity, differentiation, integration
- **Notes:** "Baby Rudin" — the standard graduate real analysis text. Famously terse and rigorous. Essential reference for measure-theoretic foundations of probability theory.
