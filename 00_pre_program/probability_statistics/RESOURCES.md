##  Courses & Lectures

- **[MIT RES.6-012 — Introduction to Probability (Spring 2018)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP60hI9ATjSFgLZpbNJ7myAg6)**
  Instructors: John Tsitsiklis, Patrick Jaillet. The gold standard intro probability course. Covers probability spaces, random variables, distributions, conditional expectation, LLN, CLT.
  [MIT OCW page](https://ocw.mit.edu/RES-6-012S18)

- **[EE 531 — Probability and Stochastic Processes (Prof. Elif Uysal)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOpr_A7B9SriE_iZmkanvUg)**
  Bridges probability into stochastic processes. Good transition into Module 1.

- **[Stanford CS109 — Introduction to Probability for Computer Scientists (2022)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLuiPz6iU5SQ8paqz9BVjGHMzzm2b-dBg_)**
  Instructor: Chris Piech. Intuitive, example-driven. Strong on MLE, Bayesian reasoning, distributions.

##  Textbooks

### [Introduction to Probability, Statistics, and Random Processes — Hossein Pishro-Nik](https://www.probabilitycourse.com/)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Hossein Pishro-Nik (UMass Amherst)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Probability spaces, random variables, distributions, expectation, limit theorems, random processes
- **Notes:** Comprehensive, peer-reviewed open-access text. Includes video lectures and interactive tools at probabilitycourse.com. Recommended starting point before measure-theoretic probability.

### [Introduction to Probability — Charles Grinstead & J. Laurie Snell](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~prob/prob/prob.pdf)
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Dartmouth College (AMS Open Math Books)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** Yes
- **Covers:** Discrete and continuous probability, distributions, central limit theorem, Markov chains
- **Notes:** Freely distributed by the American Mathematical Society. Classic text with strong conceptual grounding.

### *A First Course in Probability* — Sheldon Ross
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** Sheldon Ross (USC)
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Combinatorics, probability axioms, conditional probability, random variables, expectation, limit theorems
- **Notes:** The gold standard introductory probability text. Highly intuitive with extensive examples. Widely prescribed as the prerequisite before stochastic calculus in MFE programs.

### *Statistical Inference* — George Casella & Roger Berger
- **Type:** Textbook
- **Author/Institution:** George Casella & Roger Berger
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Free:** No
- **Covers:** Probability theory, sampling distributions, MLE, hypothesis testing, Bayesian inference, regression
- **Notes:** Graduate-level statistical inference text used in PhD qualifying exams. Dense and rigorous — ideal for understanding the statistical foundations underlying econometrics and risk models.
